## What is the Business Central Agent Playground?

The Agent Playground is a practical experimentation space for building and validating AI-assisted experiences in Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.

At a high level, it helps teams:

- Explore agent and copiloting patterns in AL-based extensions.
- Prototype natural-language interactions with business data and processes.
- Evaluate quality, safety, and performance before production rollout.
